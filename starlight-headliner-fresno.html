What you actually get with a custom starlight headliner

A starlight headliner isn't just a strip of LEDs. It's hundreds of individual fiber-optic strands hand-placed through a custom-wrapped headliner panel. Each strand is lit by a small light box mounted out of sight in your trunk or under the dash. The light box is what your phone controls.

Frequently asked questions

What is a starlight headliner?

A starlight headliner is a custom-installed ceiling lined with hundreds of fiber optic strands that look like stars. The fiber optics are powered by a small light box you can control from your phone — colors, brightness, and shooting star patterns.

How long does a starlight headliner installation take?

Most installations take 2 to 4 days depending on the car, number of stars, and whether you want shooting star modules added. We confirm timing during your quote.

Can a starlight headliner be installed in any car?

Almost any car with a removable headliner — sedans, coupes, SUVs, trucks, and even Teslas. Some headliners are easier to work with than others; we can confirm before quoting.

Do the lights connect to my phone?

Yes. Our installs include a Bluetooth controller plus an app for color changes, brightness, shooting star patterns, and music sync modes. Most setups also include a physical remote as backup.

Will it drain my car battery?

No. The fiber optic system runs off a small low-draw light source wired into your accessory power, so it only runs when the car is on. There's no parasitic battery drain.
