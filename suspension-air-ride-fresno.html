Air ride vs coilovers vs lowering springs — which is right for you?

  • Lowering springs — cheapest path to a lower stance. Good for daily drivers who just want it 1-2 inches lower. Pair with shocks or you'll feel every pothole.
  • Coilovers — the best balance of cost, performance, and adjustability. You set the height once and lock it. Better cornering than springs, smoother than a cheap drop kit.
  • Air ride — the show-car answer. Slammed at the meet, raised for the drive home, daily drivable in between. Costs more upfront but gives you everything.
  • Bagged trucks — air bags built specifically for trucks, often with notched frames and custom hard lines.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between air ride and coilovers?

Air ride uses air bags you can adjust on demand — go from slammed at a meet to street-legal ride height for the drive home. Coilovers are mechanically adjusted and locked at a chosen ride height, but offer better cornering and handling at a lower price.

Will lowering springs ruin my ride quality?

Quality lowering springs paired with the right shocks ride almost as smooth as stock. Cheap unmatched setups will feel rough — that's why we don't install no-name spring kits.

How long does a suspension install take?

Lowering springs: 1 day. Coilovers: 1-2 days. Full air ride builds: 3-5 days depending on complexity. We confirm timing during your quote.

Do you need an alignment after suspension work?

Yes. Any time you change ride height or replace suspension components, an alignment is required to prevent uneven tire wear. We coordinate this so your car leaves the shop driving straight.

Can you install parts I bought myself?

Yes. We do customer-supplied installs as long as the parts are quality brands. We'll let you know if anything looks off before installing.
