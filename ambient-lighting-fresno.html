Footwells

Frequently asked questions

What is ambient interior lighting?

Ambient interior lighting is a custom-installed LED system that adds soft accent lighting to your car's doors, dash, footwells, and trunk. The lights are RGB so you can change colors, brightness, and modes from your phone.

Will ambient lighting damage my car interior?

No. We route lights behind existing panels and trim — no cutting, no visible wires, no permanent modification. If you ever wanted to remove it, the panels would look factory.

Can I control the lights from my phone?

Yes. Our installs include a Bluetooth controller and app for color, brightness, mode (solid, fade, music sync), and zone control.

Will the lights distract me while driving?

No. Ambient lighting is designed to be soft and indirect — it accents the interior, not blast you in the face. You control brightness, and most people run it on a low setting at night.

How long does an ambient lighting install take?

Most installs take 1 to 2 days depending on the car and how many zones you want lit. We confirm timing during your quote.
