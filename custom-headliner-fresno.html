Frequently asked questions

Why is my headliner sagging?

Headliner sag happens when the foam backing between the fabric and the headliner board breaks down — usually from heat over time. Once it sags, glue alone won't fix it long-term. The headliner needs to be re-wrapped or replaced.

Can you fix a sagging headliner?

Yes. We pull the headliner board, strip off the old fabric and foam, and re-wrap it with new material. This fixes sag permanently — gluing alone is a temporary patch.

What materials do you use?

Suede, alcantara, and factory-match foam-backed headliner material. We can also do custom colors or perforated patterns if you want something different from stock.

How long does a headliner replacement take?

Most replacements take 1 to 3 days depending on the car and material. We confirm timing during your quote.

Can you add a starlight to my new headliner?

Yes — a headliner replacement is the perfect time to add fiber optic stars. Bundle the two services and save the cost of pulling the headliner twice.
