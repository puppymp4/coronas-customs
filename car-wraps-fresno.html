Frequently asked questions

How long does a vinyl wrap last in Fresno's heat?

A quality vinyl wrap from 3M or Avery Dennison lasts 5 to 7 years when washed properly and parked out of constant direct sun. The wrap actually protects your factory paint from UV damage while it's installed.

Will a wrap damage my factory paint?

No. A professional install on factory paint comes off clean and leaves the paint untouched. Repainted panels are the only risk because aftermarket paint can lift with the adhesive — we'll inspect before quoting.

How long does the install take?

Most full vinyl wraps take 3 to 5 business days from drop-off to pickup. Partial wraps like a hood or roof typically take 1 to 2 days.

How much does a full car wrap cost in Fresno?

Cost depends on car size, film color, and finish. Chrome and color-shift films cost more than standard gloss or matte. We give free, no-pressure quotes within 24 hours — call (559) 513-5505 or fill out the quote form on our homepage.

Can I wash my wrapped car?

Yes. Hand wash or touchless car wash is best. Avoid brush tunnels and high-pressure spray directly on edges or seams.

Do you wrap trucks and SUVs?

Yes. We wrap everything from compact cars to full-size trucks and SUVs — Tahoes, F-150s, Tacomas, Tesla Model Y/X, all of it.
