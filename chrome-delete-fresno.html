Frequently asked questions

What is a chrome delete?

Chrome delete is the process of covering or replacing your car's factory chrome trim with a different color — usually gloss black, satin black, or matte black. We use premium vinyl wrap that gives a clean, factory-look finish.

Will chrome delete damage my trim?

No. Vinyl chrome delete comes off cleanly and leaves the original chrome trim untouched. If you ever sell or want it back to stock, peel the vinyl off.

What parts can be chrome deleted?

Window surrounds, door handles, grille trim, badges, mirror caps, exhaust tip surrounds — almost any chrome accent on the exterior can be wrapped.

How long does chrome delete take?

Most chrome deletes take 1 day depending on how much trim you want covered. Window surrounds alone can be done in a few hours.

Can I just paint over the chrome instead?

You can, but vinyl chrome delete is reversible, faster, and cheaper. Paint requires sanding the chrome and risks lifting if not prepped perfectly. Vinyl peels off clean.
